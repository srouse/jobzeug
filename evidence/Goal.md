# Figma Role: goal and evidence framework

Created September 19, 2026.

## Goal

Build a coherent, evidence-based application for Figma's Forward Deployed Engineer role that demonstrates Scott can make design and AI tooling work within complex customer environments, then turn those solutions into reusable capabilities and product improvements.

Collect projects across Scott's career, connect them to resume roles, and select six distinct portfolio examples. Use the same factual foundation for a focused resume, an interactive webpage with substantial AI and engineering depth, a tailored cover letter, and interview preparation. The webpage should help people inspect the work and understand decisions and results. Its specific implementation remains open.

Condensed into six groups. Importance scores reflect our interpretation of the role, not Figma's official assessment or Scott's proficiency. 10 means essential; ties are intentional. These are capabilities to substantiate through projects.

Engineering includes architecture, APIs, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Customer partnership includes technical communication and enterprise constraints. AI engineering includes context, instructions, tools, orchestration, and evaluation where applicable. These details remain follow-up prompts rather than additional top-level categories.

## Applying the framework to projects

- Capture freely first; use function and skill IDs for organizing the account afterward.
- Separate capability relevance from evidence strength: a relevant project can still lack implementation or outcome details.
- Record evidence status as account only, artifact supported, outcome supported, or still unclear. Supporting artifacts and outcomes can coexist; they are not mandatory sequential levels.
- For each strong claim, identify Scott's action, a consequential decision, and an observed result. Preserve scope, collaborators, dates, limitations, and delivery status.
- One project can demonstrate several skills and functions. Reuse the evidence without counting the same outcome as separate accomplishments.
- Investigate gaps rather than inventing experience. Do not retrofit AI into older work.
- Select six complementary examples after discovery. No need for exactly one project per website theme.

## Completion criteria for the eventual application

- Six distinct, substantiated examples collectively demonstrate the central functions, with important gaps made explicit.
- Resume, website, and cover letter agree on dates, ownership, metrics, and delivery status.
- The resume establishes fit quickly; the website provides technical evidence; the cover letter explains motivation with selected supporting examples.
- Interactive elements reveal real work, decisions, or results and support the application story.
- Application files follow the project instructions, including editable DOCX and visually verified PDF where applicable.
