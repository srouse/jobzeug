# Project follow-up question bank

Use later to tighten project accounts. Ask only the few questions most useful for the current account, and let Scott narrate freely first. A project need not cover every category.

Use the [goal and skill framework](Goal.md) to identify which functions and capabilities an account supports. Choose follow-ups that establish missing evidence; do not turn the framework into a mandatory checklist for every project.

1. Where and when did this happen, and which resume role does it belong to?
2. What was the starting situation? Who needed something to change, and why did it matter?
3. How did you discover the real problem? Did you work directly with customers, partner teams, or their engineers?
4. What did you personally own, code, design, decide, or debug? What did collaborators own?
5. What technical or organizational constraints shaped the solution? What alternatives did you reject, and why?
6. How did the system work: inputs, components, APIs, data, outputs, and deployment?
7. What was the hardest failure or uncertainty, and how did you investigate and resolve it?
8. What changed from the beginning to the end? What shipped, who used it, and who maintained it?
9. What supports the outcome: artifact, customer feedback, adoption, timing, measurement, or a concrete before/after example?
10. What connection did the work have to design, tokens, components, Figma, or design-to-code workflows, if any?
11. What connection did it have to AI, agents, MCP, prompts, or automation, if any? What did the agent actually do, and how did you evaluate or constrain it?
12. What became reusable: tooling, documentation, a playbook, a component, or a product improvement? Did field feedback change the product?
13. What would you do differently now, and what demonstrates growth in your approach?
14. What can we show on an interactive webpage to let someone understand or explore the work? Which artifacts are available and appropriate to share?

Selection lens: establish actual technical ownership and results, then assess relevance to the Figma role. Do not retrofit AI into older work or imply Figma experience where the connection is only transferable.
