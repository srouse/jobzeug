## Org description

Category: Art museum / civic cultural institution

The Milwaukee Art Museum (MAM) is Wisconsin’s major art museum, on Milwaukee’s lakefront. In the early 2000s it opened the **Quadracci Pavilion**, Santiago Calatrava’s first completed building in the United States, as part of a large expansion — a high-visibility civic project of the same era as Scott’s Journal Interactive tenure.
