## Org description

Category: Bicycles / sporting goods manufacturing and retail

Trek Bicycle Corporation designs and sells bicycles and cycling gear. Official company materials state that Trek began in 1976 with a focus on high-quality craftsmanship and broadening access to cycling, and that its mission includes advancing cycling as a practical response to complex global problems. Global headquarters are in Waterloo, Wisconsin; Trek describes offices in more than twenty countries.
