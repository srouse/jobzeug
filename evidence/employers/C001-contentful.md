Company-level navigation only; role records retain their dates, source differences and detailed claims.

## Company description

Category: Content platform / enterprise software

Contentful provides a content platform that lets organizations model content as reusable structured data and deliver it across digital experiences. Its approach separates content from any single page or presentation, allowing teams to reuse text, media and other content across channels. It serves organizations coordinating content creation and implementation across marketing, editorial and engineering teams. Its composable approach supports combining content with other tools rather than treating the website as one indivisible system.

## Salesforce acquisition

Verified September 19, 2026 from Salesforce primary sources.

- **Agreement signed:** May 29, 2026, according to Salesforce’s Form S-8.
- **Public announcement:** June 1, 2026.
- **Completed:** September 1, 2026, confirmed by both Salesforce’s announcement update and Form S-8.
- **Disclosed agreement consideration:** Approximately $1.5 billion in cash, net of the value of shares already owned by Salesforce and subject to customary purchase-price adjustments, according to its July 31, 2026 Form 10-Q. This is the pre-close disclosed consideration, not a verified final adjusted closing amount.
- **Strategic rationale:** Salesforce described adding Contentful’s structured content and composable APIs to its content layer for Headless 360, Customer 360 and Agentforce. This is the buyer’s stated integration strategy, not evidence that every planned integration has shipped.
- **Customer scale at announcement:** More than 4,800 brands, updating the separately dated 4,200+ customer figure from March 2025.

Suggested short descriptor: **Contentful, acquired by Salesforce in September 2026.** For expanded context: **Salesforce completed its acquisition of Contentful in September 2026, following an agreement disclosing approximately $1.5 billion in cash consideration.** Keep the precise consideration qualifications with financial details.

This occurred during Scott’s recorded Senior Product Architect tenure. It establishes company context, not personal responsibility for the transaction. Use “Contentful, a Salesforce company” for present-day context without retroactively relabeling earlier roles as Salesforce employment.
