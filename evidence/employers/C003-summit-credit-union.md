## Company description

Category: Member-owned financial cooperative

Summit Credit Union is a Wisconsin financial cooperative owned by its members. It provides products including checking accounts, home and home-equity loans, certificates and business banking, alongside financial education. Its stated purpose centers on financial security and wealth building, with an emphasis on women’s financial wellbeing. Founded as CUNA Credit Union in 1935, it distinguishes its cooperative model through member governance and the return of benefits to members and communities.
