## Company description

Category: Insurance group / property and casualty

American Family Insurance is a Madison, Wisconsin-based insurance organization with multiple operating companies. Its business encompasses the functions required to develop, distribute and support insurance, including underwriting, actuarial work, agency relationships and claims. The group includes American Family Mutual Insurance Company and subsidiaries, American Family Connect, Homesite and Main Street America in its March 2026 company overview. This makes it a multi-company organization rather than a single standalone digital product.
