# C007: OpenHomes

Company-level navigation only; role records retain their dates, source differences and detailed claims.

## Company description

Category: Real-estate technology / historical startup

OpenHomes developed online tools for people buying and selling homes directly. The 2013 gener8tor launch announcement describes a platform supporting showing appointments, buyer questions, feedback and offers from mobile devices. Its aim was to make owner-led home transactions easier and less expensive, combining direct buyer/seller connections with transaction tools. OpenHomes participated in gener8tor’s summer 2013 accelerator program.
