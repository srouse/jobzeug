# C008: Propeller Health

Company-level navigation only; role records retain their dates, source differences and detailed claims.

## Company description

Category: Digital health / connected respiratory care

Propeller Health developed connected health tools for people with asthma and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. Its platform combined inhaler-attached sensors with a mobile application to record medication use and provide feedback and insights. The system connected a physical treatment device with software intended to support patients and clinicians in understanding respiratory care. ResMed announced an acquisition agreement in December 2018; its subsequent filing records completion in January 2019.
