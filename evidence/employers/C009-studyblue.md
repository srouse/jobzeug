## Company description

Category: Education technology / digital study tools

StudyBlue was a digital study platform where students could create their own learning materials, including flashcards and collections of concepts, and find study support in a shared content library. It tracked learning progress to help students identify gaps in their understanding. Chegg acquired StudyBlue in July 2018 to expand its study content and flashcard offering. Its central product model combined student-created materials, shared educational content and individualized study practice.
