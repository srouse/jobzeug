## Company description

Category: Social marketing / SaaS (historical)

Shoutlet was a Madison, Wisconsin–based enterprise social marketing platform for publishing, engaging, and measuring campaigns on networks such as Facebook, Twitter, and YouTube. Contemporary coverage described social CRM, campaign builders, analytics, and enterprise multi-brand controls. Customers named in acquisition-era reporting included brands such as Best Buy, Four Seasons, and Lowe’s.
