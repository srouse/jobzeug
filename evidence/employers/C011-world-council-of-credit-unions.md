## Company description

Category: International trade association / cooperative finance

World Council of Credit Unions (WOCCU) is the global trade association representing credit unions and financial cooperatives internationally. It advocates before international standard-setters, runs member education and technical assistance programs, and publishes global movement statistics. Headquarters / public presence: [woccu.org](https://www.woccu.org/).
