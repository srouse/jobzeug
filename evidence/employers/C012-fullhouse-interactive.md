## Company description

Category: Interactive / digital marketing agency

FullHouse Interactive (also styled Fullhouse) was a Milwaukee-based interactive marketing agency delivering experience-based B2B and B2C digital programs (web, mobile, Flash-era work). Later public profiles described Fortune 1000 clients including MillerCoors / Miller brands, Kimberly-Clark, GE Healthcare, and Harley-Davidson, and expansion into Chicago around 2010.

## Timeframe and limits

Scott’s LinkedIn tenure is **Jul 2005–Dec 2005**. Headcount and client lists from ~2009–2010 job postings and press are **later** than that six-month stint. LinkedIn description ties work to Miller High Life “Girl in the Moon” Flash / SEO campaign—treat as Scott’s source claim pending artifacts.
