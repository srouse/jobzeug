## Company description

Category: Metropolitan daily newspaper / digital news

The Milwaukee Journal Sentinel is Wisconsin’s major metro daily (print + digital), historically published by Journal Communications / Journal Sentinel Inc. and later part of Gannett. Scott’s LinkedIn places him on the Journal Interactive team in **2001–2002**.
