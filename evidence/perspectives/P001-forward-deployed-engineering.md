# P001: Forward-deployed engineering — bring experience to the actual situation

Captured September 19, 2026. Source: Scott’s conversational account in this task. Status: perspective captured; wording and supporting examples still developing. This is a personal interpretation, not researched market history or a completed project case study.

## Faithful summary of Scott’s perspective

Scott sees forward-deployed engineering as a particularly relevant expression of his consulting, customer-engagement and hands-on building experience. He describes decades of consulting experience and work with Contentful customers and prospects where engagement led to things being built and brought to fruition. Specific engagements and outcomes still need to be captured.

For him, the value extends beyond advice, a sales interaction or an isolated service deliverable. It involves going deeply into the customer’s workflow and making meaningful integrations work in that particular environment. His view is that AI enables an experienced practitioner to reach further into a problem and have greater implementation impact.

The work is messy and situation-specific. An effective practitioner brings broad experience and adapts to the reality in front of them, rather than imposing a predetermined philosophy. Technical capability is only part of this: Scott also emphasizes succeeding within organizational politics and relationships.

His account also raises the importance of being able to leave the engagement. The intended meaning needs clarification: leaving the customer with a sustainable capability, carrying reusable learning forward, or both. Do not treat either interpretation as confirmed yet.

## Original wording worth preserving

## Editorial interpretation, not a quotation

A possible central idea: experience provides the judgment to understand a customer’s particular constraints; AI expands what can be implemented; successful delivery requires both technical integration and organizational trust.

This could frame the website introduction, a short viewpoint essay, the cover letter’s motivation, or interview answers. It should be supported by projects rather than presented as proof of results by itself.
