# P002: Build deep to influence large initiatives

Captured September 20, 2026. Source: Scott’s conversational account in this task. Status: perspective with strong State Farm worked example; more multi-year examples still welcome. This is a personal operating principle, not a project case study by itself.

## Faithful summary of Scott’s perspective

For the last **three-plus years**, Scott describes operating by **building real things that go quickly and deep** into a problem space. Those builds **reveal the strengths and weaknesses of much larger initiatives**. Influence comes from getting hands around the work — not only from critique in the abstract.

Because he is both a **designer and a developer**, he can **experiment with ways to solve** the problems he finds: proactive critique **plus** clear examples of solutions, then **telling those stories**. He calls this combination his **absolute superpower**, expressed again and again.

### The template (clarified with State Farm)

He does **not** need to own the final design system or the production codebase. He builds **enough that is specific** to the organization — token structures, components, working code — so people can **see exactly what he is talking about**. Someone else still has to design “the” system; someone else still has to own the long-term code. Where the rubber hits the road: he stays **four or five steps ahead**, primarily because he **already has the code (and design) in hand**. When anyone has a question, he can give a **concrete example**, or he has already prepared a **presentation that proactively introduces the idea**. At State Farm, he says **that template played out perfectly**.

## Editorial interpretation, not a quotation

Working labels:

- **Prototype-as-critique** — a deep, fast artifact that makes the larger program’s gaps and better paths visible.
- **Lead without owning the end state** — influence by being ahead with proof, not by holding permanent design or eng ownership.
- **Answer-ready** — questions get a concrete example or a proactive deck, not a slideware-only opinion.

Distinct from P001’s forward-deployed customer-environment focus, though both stress hands-on building over abstract advice.
