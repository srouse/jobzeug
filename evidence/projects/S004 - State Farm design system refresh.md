## Account summary (spine only)

At State Farm, design-system progress was blocked by politics, split org structure (DS design vs DS development), and weak brand-decision guidance. Scott’s short tenure combined three moves:

1. **Persuade** senior management that tokens were necessary ([S005](S005%20-%20State%20Farm%20tokens%20persuasion.md)).
2. **Build** the Figma-side system — tokens, components, repo-sync plugin — and raise systematic Figma practice with senior designers, including deep collaboration with a Google design-system expert ([S006](S006%20-%20State%20Farm%20Figma%20design%20system.md)).
3. **Enable** the development team on Lit/web components and act as a trusted peer bridge so both sides had to deal in truth ([S007](S007%20-%20State%20Farm%20Lit%20engineering%20bridge.md)).

The umbrella claim: working prototypes and peer credibility made the narrative land; the narrative unlocked adoption; the bridge and plugin kept the right decisions in the right hands while engineering kept the system coherent. Tokens “happened” because all three legs moved together.
