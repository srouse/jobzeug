## Account summary

<a id="source-account"></a>

Summary of Scott’s September 27, 2026 account (not a transcript). He titled this **Design System Guidance**.

He says this was the **other half** of the job, in addition to the R&D work. He was there to help **build out a design system**.

**Who employed him.** American Family owned a number of companies. One company oversaw all of them. He worked for that overarching company. On September 27, 2026 he added that the overarching company was **also called American Family**, and that there was a separate **American Family brand that sold insurance** underneath it. They also owned **The General**, **HomeSite Insurance**, and **Main Street America**, and each had its own brand.

The employer research (company facts, March 2026) already lists Homesite and Main Street America inside the group, spelled Homesite. It does not list The General. That research is later org context, not his 2019–2020 account.

**What he was asked to do.** Analyze **decisions about software that was being brought in**, and how well that software would be adapted into a **reusable design system**. He also **led the initial conversations between design technologists** in order to figure out whether they could make a **centralized design system**.

He calls it a **really big project** that **outlasted his tenure**. He was generally the one leaned on to **get the conversations to happen**, to figure out **technically how to get these things to work**, and to **tell the stories to the right people** so they would not buy the wrong software or put it together the wrong way. He also **communicated between designers and developers**, which he calls a common theme in his work.

He was **hired as a designer**, and he was talking with **developers** at the same time and with **design technologists** who were **leaders within each of the different brands** at the other companies. He says it was a really big deal just to **minimally get people talking** about these things and **imagining how to get them connected**.
