## Account summary

<a id="source-account"></a>

Summary of Scott’s September 27, 2026 account (not a transcript). He asked for one catch-all. He does not want the individual startups broken into their own projects.

He says the work in this role was **primarily as a designer**. It was one of the first full-time design titles he had, **just after OpenHomes**. He also **worked extensively with startups** during this time.

**What he was asked to do.** Come in and **help consult on the business model relative to software**. He did **not** come up with the idea, and he did not help them carve the idea itself. He was **deeply involved** in helping them understand **how it could be possible to put that into an application**, and in figuring out **how to get that validated along the way**, so they would not spend or waste too much time early and miss market fit.

**Volume.** He says he did **dozens and dozens** of these. That count is his characterization, not a counted list.

**How he prototyped.** He prototyped in **HTML** and **React**, and also **iOS**, which at the time was **Objective-C and Swift**. On September 27, 2026 he confirmed React. The first telling had been “React primarily probably.” He also did **full-resolution prototypes**. He calls that a really great learning experience, because full resolution **fails and wins for different reasons than low resolution**. He says he had **experimented with all of them** at the time.

**Outcomes.** He worked with a lot of **really successful** startups and a lot of **unsuccessful** ones, which he calls an extremely important learning curve. Some were **worth hundreds of millions**. Some **flashed out really quickly** and did not even get funding. Those are his descriptions of the companies, not a valuation he measured and not a count of wins and losses.

**Domains.** A whole variety. He named **healthcare**, **finance**, and **education**. He says education is a big thing in Madison.

**What a presentation would be.** If he gave one, he would show **a whole bunch of logos** and **designs he did during that time**. He later said not to put any names on this project at this point. No logos or design files are attached.
