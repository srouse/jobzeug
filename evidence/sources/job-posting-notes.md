# Figma Forward Deployed Engineer

Source: https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/figma/jobs/6158162004
Read September 17–18, 2026. Condensed reference, not a complete posting archive.

Senior/staff engineering position in Product and Engineering on a founding team. US remote or US hub; periodic customer travel.

Work combines enterprise customer discovery, hands-on integration and product engineering, design-system adoption, and reusable solutions. Technical areas include tokens, naming, guidelines, Code Connect, evaluations, Make Local, Code Layers, unfamiliar stacks, git/CI, MCP, agent skills and prompts. Customer findings should influence product direction and shipped improvements.

Requirements include five or more years delivering production software, customer/partner collaboration, engineering adaptability, design-to-code experience, TypeScript/JavaScript or Python, APIs/SDKs, agentic coding, and enterprise security/access constraints.

Solutions engineering and similar customer-facing technical backgrounds are explicitly valued, alongside developer tooling and cross-functional enterprise work.

Application requires a resume and a three-to-four-sentence explanation of why Figma. Additional information can include a cover letter. Core evidence to establish: production ownership, customer discovery, reusable implementation, agent reliability, and enterprise constraints.
